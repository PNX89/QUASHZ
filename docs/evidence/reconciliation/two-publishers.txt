US dollars per euro, 2024-01-02 to 2026-08-21, as published by the
Federal Reserve and by the European Central Bank:

  days both publish            651
  days they agree exactly      10
  median absolute difference   0.0015
  standard deviation           0.00273
  largest difference           0.0151
  days only the ECB publishes  23
  days only the Fed publishes  11

Neither is wrong. They are fixed at different times of day, so a rule demanding
equality rejects a good series and a rule with no tolerance accepts a stale one.

The Federal Reserve also publishes the spread between its own ten year and two
year yields. Over 12,557 days on which all three exist it equals the
difference on 12,554 and disagrees on 3:

  1990-11-21   8.27 - 7.55 = 0.72, published 0.74
  1991-01-29   8.03 - 7.1 = 0.93, published 0.95
  1995-11-29   5.85 - 5.42 = 0.43, published 0.42

Those days are kept rather than filtered. A tolerance tuned until nothing fails
has been tuned to the sample it was tested on.
